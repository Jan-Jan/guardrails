## Review

### Round 1

**finding-1**: requirement, medium — Upgrade step 4 and the new Done when entry offer "list it in a plan in `docs/plans/`" as an alternative to fixing a `check-trace.sh` finding. But upgrade step 5 integrates through `merge-change`, and that path cannot complete with an exit-1 finding left in the tree: `verify-before-merge` check 2 requires `check-trace.sh` to be clean; Done when's own last bullet requires "the check scripts pass"; most upgrade findings are exit 1, not warnings (`upgrade-notes.md` says "Every derived item goes red at the first run"). So the remedy misleads in normal use. An agent that lists a red finding instead of fixing it hits a red gate at merge. Nothing is lost, because the gate still blocks the merge. The ADR's Decision has the same gap. The plan option needs to be limited to findings that do not fail the gate (warnings such as `UNRESOLVED-PR`), or to a gate switched off with a recorded reason.
disposition: fixed in `1b8321c`, refined by finding-5: upgrade step 4, Done when, upgrade-notes item 7, rationale.md and the ADR say each failure is fixed and only warnings are listed; the ADR's Context records the narrowing. The step 4 test's negation (`or list it in a plan`) and anchored step 4 line turn `not ok` when the old wording returns.

**finding-2**: requirement, low — Item 7 of the order of work in `upgrade-notes.md` is tested by nothing; it can be deleted with all four tests green. Test 2 greps across the whole of `SKILL.md`, not inside the upgrade section, so it does not check that the line is upgrade step 4.
disposition: fixed in `1b8321c`: the order-of-work test asserts item 7, and the step 4 test reads only the "Upgrading the scripts" section and anchors on the `4.` line.

**finding-3**: requirement, low — The D4 trim of the retrofit `srs.md` sentence drops two things, against the plan's claim that it "keeps both of its facts": main recommended moving "each monolithic `doc_*` file" into a directory, and the tree now says "moving it", meaning `srs.md` only; and main ended "so changes stop conflicting", which is gone and restated nowhere under `skills/ratchet/`.
