## What was wrong, and what was built

A plan carried the full code of every task step. Once merged, that code duplicated the tree and drifted from it: on 2026-10-06, 6,518 of 25,148 plan lines sat in code fences. D11 decided that plans are pruned at merge, keeping every heading and all prose so that citations of plan steps still resolve.

`scripts/prune-plans.sh` finds each task section (a `T<N>` or `Task <N>` heading, to the next heading of its level or higher), and replaces each fence in it, backtick or tilde, nested or indented, with `*(Code pruned at merge: <N> lines. Files touched: <value>.)*`. It keeps a fence with a line that opens with `red -> green`, since step 6b copies those attestations into the record after the review. It leaves a plan whole when any tracked Markdown file cites one of its lines, or a range ending at one, at or after its first pruned fence; it decides that for every plan before rewriting any, so a second run prunes nothing. By default it takes the plans the change adds or modifies against `--base`; `--all` takes every plan. `prune_plans: off` turns it off; an absent key or no config is on; any other value is exit 2.

It runs at `merge-change` step 1, after the base merge and chained on its success, and commits only `docs/plans`, so the step 2 gate measures the pruned tree. D11 named `finalize-docs.sh` at step 3; that runs after the gate, and pruning there would have sent nearly every merge through a second suite run at step 6. The maintainer ruled for step 1 and its own script; `ADR-bh4xgr` records the decision and the deviation. The reviewer is told to check the pointers and prose against the diff, `plan-change` says code in task steps is pruned, the ratchet upgrade notes name the key, and `templates/AGENTS-block.md` and `README.md` list the script. `merge-change`'s `SKILL.md` is at 2,000 words; room was made by cutting two pre-flight descriptions the script's header gives and moving explanation into its rationale.

The plans on `main` were pruned with `--all`: 27 plans, 259 blocks, 4,360 lines, then, after the base merge brought it in, `docs/plans/2026-10-07-test-seams.md`, 14 blocks, 443 lines; 28 plans in all. Four are left whole: `2026-09-04-units-implementation.md` and `2026-09-10-field-report-fixes.md`, cited from `docs/plans/2026-09-16-scan-scope.md`; `2026-09-29-agent-first-skills-change-2.md`, cited from `docs/verification/2026-09-30-agent-first-scripts.md`; and `2026-10-04-adr-ids.md`, cited from `docs/verification/2026-10-05-adr-ids.md`.
